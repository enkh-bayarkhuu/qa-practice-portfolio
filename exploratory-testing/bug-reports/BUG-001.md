# BUG-001: Smartwatch Does Not Automatically Update Time After Daylight Saving Time Change

## Environment
- Device: Apple Watch (Model A1978)
- watchOS Version: 10.6.2
- Paired Device: iPhone 17
- iOS Version: 26.5.2
- Location: Alberta, Canada
- Event: Daylight Saving Time transition
- Date: November 1, 2026

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Have an Apple Watch paired with an iPhone.
2. Wait for the automatic Daylight Saving Time transition.
3. Check the time displayed on the iPhone.
4. Check the time displayed on the Apple Watch.
5. Compare the displayed times.

## Expected Result
The Apple Watch should automatically update its displayed time after the Daylight Saving Time transition and show the correct local time.

## Actual Result
The iPhone updated its time automatically, but the Apple Watch remained one hour behind. The watch eventually synchronized after troubleshooting and resetting.

## Severity
Not assessed

## Status
Observed, not reproduced
