# TC-001: Verify That the Date of Birth Field Rejects Future Dates

## Environment
- **Website:** https://demoqa.com/automation-practice-form
- **Browser:** Google Chrome 155.0.8059.40 (arm64)
- **Operating System:** macOS Sequoia 15.3 (24D2059)

## Steps to Execute
1. Open the Practice Form Page.
2. Fill in all required fields with valid information.
3. Select **01 January,2027** in the Date of Birth field.
4. Click Submit.
5. Observe whether the form accepts the future date.

## Expected Result
The form should reject future dates in the Date of Birth field and prevent submission until a valid date is selected.

## Actual Result
The form accepted 01 January 2027, as the date of birth and allowed the registration form to be submitted successfully.

## Test Result
Fail
