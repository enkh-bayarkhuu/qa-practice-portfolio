# BUG-001: Will Enable 5 seconds button is disabled

## Environment
- Website: https://demoqa.com/dynamic-properties
- Browser: Google Chrome 155.0.8059.40 (arm64)
- Operating System: macOS Sequoia 15.3 (24D2059)

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the Dynamic Properties page.
2. Locate the button labeled Will enable 5 seconds.
3. Wait approximately 5 seconds.
4. Click the button and observe its behavior.

## Expected Result
The button should become enabled and clickable approximately 5 seconds after the page loads.

## Actual Result
The button initially appeared faded and became fully visible after approximately 5 seconds. However, clicking it produced no visible response.
