# BUG-001: Close Button Does Not Work After Form Submission

## Environment
- Website: https://demoqa.com/dynamic-properties
- Browser: Google Chrome 155.0.8059.40 (arm64)
- Operating System: macOS Sequoia 15.3 (24D2059)

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the Practice Form page.
2. Fill in all required fields with valid information.
3. Click Submit.
4. Locate the Close button at the bottom of the submission confirmation window.
5. Click Close and observe the result.
6. Click outside the confirmation window.


## Expected Result
Clicking the Close button should dismiss the submission confirmation window.

## Actual Result
Clicking the Close button did not dismiss the confirmation window. However, clicking outside the window  closed it.
