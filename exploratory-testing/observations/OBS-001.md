# OBS-001: Changes to iPad Split-Screen Window Arrangement

## Environment
- **Device:** iPad Air 11-inch (M2)
- **iPadOS Version:** 18.7.8
- **Date:** November 4, 2026

## Observation

**Previous behavior:** When opening two apps side by side, both windows automatically filled the screen. You could adjust the divider to change how much space each app used.

**Current behavior:** After the update, the windows no longer automatically fill the screen. You have to manually resize them, and even after adjusting, they may not fill the entire screen.

## User Experience

The new arrangement feels less convenient than before. It takes more effort to resize the windows, and it's harder to get the arrangement exactly how you want it.

Usability Observation
